Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who this applies to

A single person (the owner) runs mailclean for their own email accounts only. The tool is not available to the public, and no other accounts are connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, mailclean uses the Gmail API to read and organise messages in the owner's mailboxes. This covers message headers, content, labels and basic mail settings such as filters. The permissions it requests are gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic.

How the data is used

The data is used to find mail that needs attention, summarise it, suggest and carry out clean-up actions (labelling, filing, moving to Trash and unsubscribing), and send replies that the owner has approved.

Storage

Copies of message data and the sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's private server. Only the owner's user account can access them.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Excerpts of messages may be processed by the AI language-model services that the owner uses as a personal assistant (currently OpenAI and Anthropic) to summarise mail and draft replies. That processing is subject to those providers' terms.

Retention and removal

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the owner through the GitHub account that hosts this page.
