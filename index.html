mailclean

mailclean is a private email-management tool. A single person uses it for their own Gmail and Google Workspace accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other accounts are connected to it.

With the owner's permission, it finds messages that need attention, summarises them, suggests clean-up actions, files unwanted mail or moves it to Trash, and sends replies that the owner has approved.
